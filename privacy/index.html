Privacy Policy

What Gentian does with your information — based on how the app actually works.

Effective 20 November 2026 · Applies to the Gentian – Swiss Alps app and this website.

Summary

  • No account, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no crash-reporting software from third parties in the app.
  • Your bucket list, completed places, saved trips, routes, GPX files and offline packs stay on your iPhone.
  • Your precise position is used on your device. When you ask for something “near me”, Gentian sends an approximate position (rounded to about 1 km) to the data provider that answers the request.
  • To load maps, timetables, weather, avalanche and closure information, your iPhone connects directly to independent public data providers, which see your IP address and what you requested.
  • We (the developer) operate no server for the app and receive none of this data.

Who is responsible

Gentian is developed and published by Bülent İmat, an independent developer (“we”). Contact details are on the Contact page.

2. Information sent to third-party data providers

Gentian has no backend of its own. When a feature needs live data, your iPhone requests it directly from the provider below over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. Like any website, each provider receives your IP address and the request itself, and processes them under its own privacy policy and retention rules. We do not control or receive those records.

The exact set of providers is listed in the app under Guides → Settings → Data sources & attributions and on our Data sources page.

Location in detail

  • Gentian asks for location access only “While Using the App”. It never uses background location.
  • Your precise position is processed on your device. Coordinates that Gentian itself puts into requests for nearby stops, conditions or closures are rounded to two decimal places (roughly 1 km).
  • When the map shows the area around you, the map tiles for that area are downloaded from swisstopo, so the tile requests reveal the map area being viewed (not your exact coordinates).
  • Converting your position to Swiss LV95 coordinates happens on your device.

3. Things you choose to share or open

  • Sharing: when you share a destination, trip, GPX file or your SOS coordinates, the iOS share sheet sends it to the app or person you pick. We do not receive it.
  • Emergency calls: SOS buttons open your phone’s dialer after you confirm. The call is handled by your mobile network; Gentian sends no data about it.
  • Webcams and external links: webcam pages, operator websites, ticket pages and official sources open in the in-app browser (Safari View Controller) or Safari only after you tap. Those websites may use cookies or analytics under their own privacy policies. Gentian adds no personal data or location to these links.

4. Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, e-mail address, contacts, photos, health data, payment data, advertising identifiers or usage analytics, and we do not track you across apps or websites. Gentian has no user accounts. No in-app purchases are active.

5. Apple distribution and diagnostics

Apple distributes the app. If you join the TestFlight beta, Apple shares with the developer the information described in Apple’s TestFlight terms (for example your tester e-mail or name if you were invited by e-mail, the feedback and screenshots you choose to send, and crash reports if you allow sharing). If you enable Share With App Developers in iOS settings, Apple may share anonymised crash and usage diagnostics. We use this only to fix problems.

6. If you contact us

If you e-mail us or open a public issue, we receive what you send (for example your address and message) and use it only to answer you and improve the app. Public GitHub issues are visible to everyone and are governed by GitHub’s privacy statement — please don’t include personal data there.

7. This website

This website is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies, uses no analytics and loads no third-party fonts or scripts. GitHub, as the host, processes technical data such as IP addresses in server logs to deliver and secure the site (see GitHub’s privacy statement).

8. Legal bases and your rights

Where the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) or the EU/EEA General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies, the limited processing described here is based on providing the features you request (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)) and our legitimate interest in operating a working, secure app (Art. 6(1)(f)). Location is used only after you grant permission, which you can withdraw at any time in iOS settings.

You have the right to request information about, correction or deletion of personal data we hold, to object to processing and to complain to a supervisory authority (in Switzerland the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner, FDPIC). Because Gentian keeps your data on your device, you can delete it yourself at any time.

9. Children

Gentian is a general travel app and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone.

10. Changes

If the app’s data handling changes (for example a new data provider, purchases or a support service), we will update this policy before the change is released and change the effective date above. Material changes will be highlighted on this page.
