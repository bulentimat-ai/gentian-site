Support

Help, troubleshooting and how to reach us.

Contact

Need help?

For app support and bug reports, email support@gentianapp.com.

For general enquiries, email info@gentianapp.com.

TestFlight beta testers

In the TestFlight app, open Gentian and choose Send Beta Feedback, or take a screenshot in Gentian and tap Share Beta Feedback. Feedback and crash reports you choose to send go to the developer through Apple.

Public feedback and bug reports

You can open an issue in the website’s public repository: github.com/bulentimat-ai/gentian-site/issues. Issues are public — please do not post personal data, your location or anything you would not want others to read.

When you report a problem, please include your iPhone model, iOS version, the Gentian version (Guides → Settings) and the destination or screen concerned. Please don’t send personal data that isn’t needed.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use Gentian on iPhone or iPad?

Gentian is designed for iPhone. It can also be used on compatible iPads in iPhone compatibility mode, but it is not yet optimised for the iPad screen. It is currently available only to invited beta testers through TestFlight.

Gentian cannot find my location
  • Allow location access: iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Gentian → While Using the App.
  • Gentian never asks for background location; it only reads your position while the app is open.
  • GPS needs a clear view of the sky. In deep valleys, gorges, forests or buildings the position can be inaccurate or unavailable.
  • Everything except “near me” features works without location access.
What works without an internet connection?

Destination guides, photos, practical guides, your bucket list and saved trips are on your device, and the SOS screen and GPS positioning work without mobile data. Maps, timetables, weather, avalanche bulletins, closures and webcams need a connection. Offline packs keep a dated snapshot of conditions — always check its date; it is not live.

No connections are shown in the trip planner
  • Check your internet connection and try again.
  • Timetable data comes from transport.opendata.ch (Swiss open data). This community-run service can be slow or temporarily unavailable.
  • Some mountain lifts run only seasonally or are not in the national timetable; Gentian then shows the walking or lift part as an estimate.
  • Always confirm departures, platforms and operating days with the operator (e.g. the SBB app or station displays) on the day.
A webcam link does not open or shows the wrong view
  • Webcam pages belong to operators and tourism organisations. They can be offline, seasonal, slow or replaced.
  • Some sites block or delay pages; try again later or open the operator’s website directly.
  • Links marked “Unverified view” are still being confirmed. Please tell us about any wrong or broken webcam (see Contact).
Weather, avalanche or closure information looks outdated

Each card shows its source and update time. Providers publish on their own schedules (the SLF avalanche bulletin, for example, is updated at fixed times). Gentian does not monitor conditions continuously and sends no alerts. Check the official sources linked in the app before you set off.

How do I import or export a GPX file?

Open Guides → Maps & GPS, tap the route button and choose import (from the Files app) or export. Exported files are shared through the iOS share sheet to the app or person you choose.

How do I delete my data?

Gentian stores your data only on your device. Use Guides → Settings → Reset app data, or delete the app. There is no account and nothing for us to delete on a server.

Is Gentian free?

During beta testing Gentian is free to testers. Pricing for the public release has not been decided; nothing can be purchased in the app today.

Is Gentian an official SBB, swisstopo, MeteoSwiss, SLF or Rega app?

No. Gentian is an independent app that uses public open-data services and links to official sources. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by any of these organisations or any cable-car or railway operator.

Safety

In an emergency in Switzerland call 112 (European emergency number) or 1414 (Rega air rescue; +41 333 333 333 from a foreign phone). Gentian’s SOS screen helps you dial and read out your coordinates, but it cannot place calls without mobile coverage and does not alert anyone automatically. Information in Gentian does not replace official warnings, operator notices or your own judgement — see the Terms of Use.
