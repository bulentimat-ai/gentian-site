Terms of Use & Safety Information

Please read these before relying on Gentian in the mountains.

Effective 20 November 2026 · Gentian – Swiss Alps (iPhone, beta)

1. About Gentian

Gentian is an independent travel guide and planning tool for the Swiss Alps, developed by Bülent İmat. By using the app you agree to these terms. Apple’s Licensed Application End User License Agreement (EULA) also applies to apps downloaded from the App Store; during beta testing, Apple’s TestFlight terms apply as well.

2. Information, not instructions

The Top 100 ranking, descriptions, durations, difficulty ratings, seasons, tips and family or dog notes are editorial opinions and estimates. They are not official information from any tourism organisation, operator or authority, and conditions on the ground change.

3. Mountain hazards

Mountain travel and hiking involve real risks: changing weather, avalanches, rockfall, ice, steep and exposed terrain, high altitude, sun and cold. You are responsible for your own decisions, equipment, fitness and route choice. When in doubt, turn back, choose an easier option or go with a qualified mountain guide.

4. Weather and avalanche information

  • Forecasts are model predictions for a point and altitude and can be wrong, especially in mountains.
  • Gentian’s weather “cautions” are generated from forecasts. They are not official warnings; official warnings are published by MeteoSwiss.
  • The avalanche bulletin describes the general danger in a whole warning region. A low danger level does not mean a slope is safe. Read the full bulletin from SLF and follow local signs and closures.
  • Gentian does not monitor conditions continuously and does not send alerts.

5. Trail closures

Closure information covers the official SwitzerlandMobility network as published by the authorities. Other paths, local closures, construction, livestock or hunting restrictions may not be included. Absence of a closure in the app does not mean a trail is open or safe.

6. Trip planning, timetables and lifts

  • Connections come from the Swiss open-data timetable via a community-run service with no guaranteed availability. Timetables, platforms and operating days can change at short notice.
  • Walking times, last-mile segments and time on site are estimates, not operator schedules.
  • Cable cars, cog railways and boats can be closed for maintenance, weather, wind or season, and may require reservations. Check the operator’s notices on the day.
  • Gentian does not sell tickets or make reservations; ticket links lead to the operators.

7. Maps, GPS and offline use

GPS positions can be inaccurate or unavailable in valleys, forests, gorges and near rock faces. Drawn routes are straight lines between points, not snapped to trails. Route following shows distance and deviation only and is not turn-by-turn navigation. Map tiles and live data need an internet connection; offline packs contain a dated snapshot, not live information. Carry a paper map or other backup on serious routes.

8. Webcams

Webcam links lead to pages run by operators and tourism organisations. Gentian does not control their availability, camera position or image freshness and cannot confirm when an image was taken. Some beta links are marked “Unverified view”. A webcam image does not tell you whether a route is safe.

9. Emergency features

The SOS screen helps you dial Swiss emergency numbers and read out or share your coordinates. It does not contact rescue services automatically, does not monitor you, and cannot work without mobile coverage or battery. It does not replace a satellite messenger, a mountain rescue plan or telling someone your route.

Gentian uses data from independent providers (see Data sources) and is not affiliated with SBB, swisstopo, MeteoSwiss, SLF, Rega, Switzerland Tourism, or any railway, cable-car or tourism operator. Their names are used only to identify the source of information. External websites are subject to their own terms.

The app is provided “as is” during beta testing. Features may change, be interrupted or be removed, and we may update these terms; the effective date above will change when we do. Pricing for a public release has not been decided, and no purchases are offered in the app today.

12. Liability

To the extent permitted by applicable law, we are not liable for damage resulting from reliance on information in the app, from unavailable or incorrect third-party data, or from decisions made in the mountains. Nothing in these terms limits liability that cannot be limited by law, including for intent or gross negligence.
