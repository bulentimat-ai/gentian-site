Contact

Questions, feedback, corrections and bug reports.

Support e-mail coming soon. A dedicated support address will be published here before Gentian is released publicly on the App Store.

TestFlight beta testers

In the TestFlight app, open Gentian and choose Send Beta Feedback, or take a screenshot in Gentian and tap Share Beta Feedback. Feedback and crash reports you choose to send go to the developer through Apple.

Public feedback and bug reports

You can open an issue in the website’s public repository: github.com/bulentimat-ai/gentian-site/issues. Issues are public — please do not post personal data, your location or anything you would not want others to read.

Corrections to destination information or webcams

If a destination description, a seasonal note, an operator link or a webcam is wrong, please tell us which destination and what you saw. Corrections are reviewed before they are included in an app update.

Gentian is developed by Bülent İmat, an independent developer. Gentian has no customer accounts and no call centre.
